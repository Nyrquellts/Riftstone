Riftstone Blade (display face for Studio's wordmark and headings)

riftstone-blade.woff2 is a subset of Riftstone Blade Regular 0.300: printable ASCII, Latin-1, common
typographic punctuation and only the Japanese characters Studio's headings use. tools/studio_font.py
makes it from the full face (RiftstoneBlade-Regular.ttf).

Its Latin letters are original Riftstone designs; its Japanese characters are carved from Noto Serif JP,
and a few supplemental symbols come from Noto Serif and Noto Sans Math. The font is licensed under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (no Reserved Font Name). The licence texts of its sources travel with
it here: NotoSerifJP-OFL.txt, NotoSerif-OFL.txt, NotoSansMath-OFL.txt.
